# Community code of conduct

ReaderMD should be a place where careful work and ambitious ideas feel safe to
share.

## Our standard

Be kind, concrete, and curious.

- Welcome people regardless of background, identity, experience, language, or
  role.
- Critique ideas and changes, never a person's worth or intelligence.
- Give feedback that is specific enough to act on.
- Make room for questions and admit uncertainty.
- Credit other people's work and respect licenses, privacy, and boundaries.
- Take responsibility when your words or actions cause harm.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, sustained disruption, insults, doxxing, impersonation, deliberate
misrepresentation, or publishing another person's private information without
permission.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository activity, discussions, reviews, project
events, and public spaces where someone is representing ReaderMD.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[adam@jesion.pl](mailto:adam@jesion.pl). Do not use the security-reporting
channel for ordinary technical disagreement.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. The project steward may
edit or remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, temporarily
suspend access, or permanently ban someone depending on severity and pattern.
Enforcement should protect affected people, give clear reasons where safety
allows, and apply the same standard to maintainers and contributors.

Technical disagreement, critical review, or declining a patch is not by itself
a conduct violation. How we disagree still matters.
